About the Workshop

The Data Agents over Heterogeneous and Evolving Data workshop aims to establish insight discovery from heterogeneous and evolving data as a focused research agenda at the intersection of machine learning, natural language processing, data systems, human-computer interaction, and AI for science.

Unlike workshops centered on table representation, query generation, generic agents, or domain-specific AI applications, this workshop centers the full insight-discovery lifecycle: evidence acquisition, reasoning, tool use, verification, memory, adaptation, and decision support.

It will benefit researchers and practitioners studying data-centric reasoning, retrieval, memory, tool use, verification, evaluation, human-agent collaboration, and trustworthy AI systems.

We organize the workshop around three primary questions:

  1. How can data agents discover, align, and integrate evidence across structured, unstructured, and multimodal sources that are fragmented, inconsistent, and partially observed?
  2. How can data agents reason over evolving data, while maintaining memory of schemas, assumptions, and prior findings, and adapting as distributions drift, semantics shift, and user goals change, without silently invalidating earlier conclusions?
  3. How can data agents ground insights in provenance and recomputable evidence, quantify uncertainty, proactively surface emerging patterns, and support human decision-making so that their outputs are trustworthy enough to act on?

These questions map onto four themes that structure our call for papers and program: (i) agentic reasoning and tool use; (ii) memory, adaptation, and self-evolution; (iii) search, grounding, and verification over heterogeneous sources; and (iv) benchmarks and evaluation for trustworthy, proactive insight discovery.

Call for Papers

Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions on topics including but not limited to:

  • Agentic reasoning and tool use

    • Data agents that transform heterogeneous data into trustworthy, reusable, and actionable insights.
    • Planning, decomposition, and tool use for querying, cleaning, analysis, visualization, simulation, and experiment execution.
    • Proactive discovery of patterns, anomalies, hypotheses, risks, and opportunities across evolving data ecosystems.
    • Applications in science, healthcare, finance, climate, education, public policy, enterprise analytics, and decision support.

  • Memory, adaptation, and self-evolution

    • Memory for schemas, metrics, user preferences, domain knowledge, prior analyses, validated assumptions, and discovered insights.
    • Adaptation to schema changes, metric drift, shifting data distributions, evolving semantics, and changing user goals.
    • Self-improvement from feedback, execution traces, human corrections, reusable skills, and past analytical workflows.

  • Search, Grounding, and Verification

    • Search-augmented reasoning, data discovery, semantic retrieval, source selection, schema linking, and cross-source evidence synthesis.
    • Grounding insights in provenance, citations, lineage, executable evidence, retrieved sources, and computed results.
    • Verification of reasoning, analytical claims, generated code, SQL, charts, hypotheses, recommendations, and proactive insights.

  • Benchmarks and Evaluation

    • Datasets and benchmarks for insight discovery across domains, modalities, interaction settings, and evolving data conditions.
    • Metrics for insight quality, novelty, actionability, planning, search, memory, adaptation, grounding, verification, robustness, and decision quality.
    • Human-agent workflows for clarification, validation, feedback, steering, transparency, privacy, fairness, accountability, and auditability.

Submission Details

We invite submissions up to 9 pages following NeurIPS workshop formatting guidelines. Reviews will be double-blind via OpenReview. Workshop publications are non-archival; accepted papers will be featured on the workshop website and presented as posters.
