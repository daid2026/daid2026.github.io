About the Workshop

Data agents are emerging as a new paradigm for insight discovery from heterogeneous and evolving data. Rather than one-shot query generation or isolated table QA, real-world data-centric problems require agents that can search across structured, unstructured, and multimodal sources; reason through multi-step analyses; use analytical tools; maintain memory of schemas, assumptions, and prior findings; adapt to changing data distributions and semantics; verify claims against evidence; and proactively surface new patterns, anomalies, or opportunities.

This workshop establishes a focused research agenda for trustworthy insight discovery at the intersection of ML, NLP, data systems, HCI, and AI for science. It centers the full lifecycle of insight discovery: evidence acquisition, reasoning, tool use, verification, memory, adaptation, and decision support.

We organize the workshop around three primary questions: (1) how data agents discover and integrate evidence from structured, unstructured, and multimodal sources; (2) how they reason over evolving data, maintain memory of prior analyses and semantics, and adapt to changing distributions, schemas, and user goals; and (3) how they verify insights, quantify uncertainty, proactively surface emerging patterns, and support downstream decision-making.

Call for Papers

Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions on topics including but not limited to:

  • Agentic Workflows for Insight Discovery

    • Data agents that transform heterogeneous data into trustworthy, reusable, and actionable insights.
    • Planning, decomposition, and tool use for querying, cleaning, analysis, visualization, simulation, and experiment execution.
    • Proactive discovery of patterns, anomalies, hypotheses, risks, and opportunities across evolving data ecosystems.
    • Applications in science, healthcare, finance, climate, education, public policy, enterprise analytics, and decision support.

  • Memory, Adaptation, and Self-Evolution for Insight Discovery

    • Memory for schemas, metrics, user preferences, domain knowledge, prior analyses, validated assumptions, and discovered insights.
    • Adaptation to schema changes, metric drift, shifting data distributions, evolving semantics, and changing user goals.
    • Self-improvement from feedback, execution traces, human corrections, reusable skills, and past analytical workflows.

  • Search, Grounding, and Verification for Reliable Insights

    • Search-augmented reasoning, data discovery, semantic retrieval, source selection, schema linking, and cross-source evidence synthesis.
    • Grounding insights in provenance, citations, lineage, executable evidence, retrieved sources, and computed results.
    • Verification of reasoning, analytical claims, generated code, SQL, charts, hypotheses, recommendations, and proactive insights.

  • Benchmarks, Evaluation, and Human Collaboration for Insight Quality

    • Datasets and benchmarks for insight discovery across domains, modalities, interaction settings, and evolving data conditions.
    • Metrics for insight quality, novelty, actionability, planning, search, memory, adaptation, grounding, verification, robustness, and decision quality.
    • Human-agent workflows for clarification, validation, feedback, steering, transparency, privacy, fairness, accountability, and auditability.

Submission Details

We invite submissions up to 9 pages following NeurIPS workshop formatting guidelines. Reviews will be double-blind via OpenReview. Workshop publications are non-archival; accepted papers will be featured on the workshop website and presented as posters.
